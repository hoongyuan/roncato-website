About Roncato Sdn Bhd

A Kuala Lumpur-based company building trusted bag brands for Malaysian customers.

Who we are

A Malaysian house of brands.

Roncato Sdn Bhd is the official Malaysian license holder for two international brands: Pierre Cardin and Hummer.

Our team handles every step of the business in-house: product design and purchasing, warehousing and distribution, retail partnerships, e-commerce, live selling and marketing. Keeping these together lets us control quality, keep prices fair and serve customers wherever they prefer to shop.
