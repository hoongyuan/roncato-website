About01

A Malaysian house of brands.

Roncato Sdn Bhd is a Kuala Lumpur-based company and the official Malaysian license holder for Pierre Cardin bags and accessories. We also build and grow Hummer, our brand for everyday carry.

Our team runs every step in-house: design and purchasing, warehousing, retail partnerships, e-commerce, live selling and marketing. That way we bring well-made, well-priced bags to customers across Malaysia.
