About us

A Malaysian company built on brand partnerships.

Roncato Sdn Bhd is a Kuala Lumpur-based company and the official Malaysian license holder for Pierre Cardin bags and accessories. We also build and grow Hummer, our brand for men's bags and everyday carry.

Our team handles every step: product design and purchasing, warehousing, retail partnerships, e-commerce, and marketing. That lets us bring well-made, well-priced products to customers nationwide.
